# Audio sources and licences

Everything under `public/audio/` is generated by the pipelines documented in `docs/brand/audio/`:
since 2026-11-07 the voice lines, sound effects and music all come from the owner's ElevenLabs
workspace on the paid Creator plan (commercial licence for generated audio), post-processed by this
project's own code. No third-party recordings, sample packs or SoundFonts are in the shipped files,
and no real person's voice is used anywhere.

## Voice lines (`vo/*.m4a`) — ElevenLabs cast (shipped 2026-11-07)

- **Speech synthesis:** ElevenLabs, through the owner's workspace on the **Creator plan** (paid;
  upgraded 2026-11-07 before any shipped clip was rendered). Under ElevenLabs' terms a paid
  subscription grants a commercial licence to the generated audio; free-tier output (non-commercial,
  attribution required) was never shipped — see the history below.
- **Voices:** one voice per character, designed from a text prompt with the Voice Design model
  `eleven_ttv_v3` (language `ko`) and saved in the workspace. They are synthetic voices: no real
  person, no library voice, no voice clone of a recording. Voice ids are recorded per character in
  `docs/brand/audio/lines.json` (`elevenlabs.voice_id`); the character concept each was designed to
  is `concept` there.
- **Renders:** every line was rendered with `eleven_v4` from the directed prompt in `lines.json`
  (`v4`: audio tags such as `[whispers]`, `[cold]`, `[cackles]` plus the spoken Korean text), two
  takes per line (one line has a single take: its second generation failed on the server). Generation
  ids per line: `docs/brand/audio/v4/gens.tsv` (the takes themselves live in the ElevenLabs workspace
  history, flow `Ss9pzZgHVpHjc8M3vTOY`; they are not in the repository).
- **Post-processing** (`docs/brand/audio/v4/master_v4.py`: trim, 60 Hz high-pass, Rakaeli's
  transformed-form detune / octave layers, Michelle's +2 semitone lift, Midos' slap echo, -16 LUFS
  normalisation, a transient limiter, AAC-LC 64 kb/s 32 kHz mono) is original numpy code plus
  ffmpeg 6.1 (rubberband, alimiter, aecho filters) and `pyloudnorm` 0.2.0 (MIT), used as offline
  tools; nothing from them is embedded in the shipped files. QA per clip: `v4/qa-v4.json`.

### History

- **Qwen3-TTS cast (2026-11-03 → 2026-11-07, replaced):** the previous clips were made with
  `Qwen/Qwen3-TTS-12Hz-1.7B-VoiceDesign` and `-Base` (both Apache-2.0) by cloning designed
  references in `docs/brand/audio/refs/` (also VoiceDesign output, no real person). The owner judged
  them not good enough to ship and they were switched off on 2026-11-07, then replaced the same day.
  The references and scripts stay in the repository as history; no shipped clip comes from them.
- **ElevenLabs free-tier attempts (2026-11-03 and 2026-11-05, nothing shipped):** three designed
  voices were saved (Brutile, Sad, Marki; later reused on the paid plan) and a few rampid lines were
  rendered with library voices ("taemin", "Min-jun", "Deok") before ElevenLabs disabled free-tier
  generation for the account. Those takes were free-tier (non-commercial) output in library voices
  that belong to real voice actors; they were kept outside the repository and never shipped.

## Sound effects (`sfx/*.m4a`) — ElevenLabs (shipped 2026-11-07)

- **Generation:** ElevenLabs Sound Effects, model `eleven_text_to_sound_v2`, on the owner's Creator
  plan (commercial licence), through the ElevenLabs connector. One prompt per effect
  (`docs/brand/audio/sfx-v2/prompts.json`: the sound, its texture and space, 0.5-1.8 s), two takes
  each; four effects whose first takes came out almost silent were re-rendered with reworded prompts.
  Generation ids per take: `sfx-v2/gens.tsv` (the takes live in the workspace history, flow
  `hPMT5V9ngdzZBGXWqnJ2`; not in the repository).
- **Post-processing** (`sfx-v2/master_sfx.py`, reusing the voice pipeline): take choice, trim,
  -17 LUFS, transient limiter, decoded true peak under -1.5 dBTP, AAC-LC 48 kHz mono 64 kb/s. QA per
  effect in `sfx-v2/qa-sfx-v2.json`.
- **History:** the previous set (2026-11-02 → 2026-11-07) was synthesised from oscillators and seeded
  noise by `docs/brand/audio/gen_sfx.py` (original numpy/scipy code, kept as history; nothing
  shipped comes from it any more).

## Music (`bgm/*.m4a`: the six arena loops and the lobby theme) — ElevenLabs Music (shipped 2026-11-07)

- **Generation:** ElevenLabs Music, model `eleven_music_v2_5`, instrumental, 100 s per take, two
  takes per track, on the owner's Creator plan (commercial licence). Prompts (genre, mood, key,
  tempo, instruments, "steady energy, no intro, no ending") are recorded with the generation ids in
  `docs/brand/audio/bgm-v2/gens.tsv` and the flow `hPMT5V9ngdzZBGXWqnJ2`. No existing music was
  referenced in the prompts beyond genre and instrument names.
- **Loop cutting and mastering** (`docs/brand/audio/bgm-v2/make_loops.py`, original code with
  librosa 1.0 (ISC) for beat tracking and features, numpy, pyloudnorm 0.2.0 (MIT), ffmpeg 6.1 for the
  limiter and AAC): the best-matching bar-aligned 60-80 s loop of either take, its last 1.5 s
  crossfaded into the bar before the loop start, -20 LUFS, -1.6 dBFS true-peak ceiling, AAC-LC
  48 kHz stereo 65-80 kb/s with the 0.512 s guard pads `src/audio/music.ts` expects. Loop points:
  `docs/brand/audio/bgm/loops.json`.
- **History:** the previous seven loops (2026-11-05 → 2026-11-07) were composed in Python and rendered
  with FluidSynth and the MIT-licensed FluidR3_GM SoundFont (`docs/brand/audio/bgm/tracks.py`,
  `bgm_lib.py`, `build_bgm.py`; kept as history). No shipped file contains those samples any more,
  so the SoundFont's MIT notice no longer applies to the shipped audio.

## Encoding

AAC-LC in an MP4 container (`.m4a`) via ffmpeg's native `aac` encoder: voice 32 kHz mono 64 kb/s,
SFX 48 kHz mono 64 kb/s, music 48 kHz stereo 65–80 kb/s. Decodes in Chromium (Android WebView) and Safari/WKWebView.
